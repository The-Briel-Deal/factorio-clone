* Factorio's terrain textures are the following dimensions:
  * The top solid rectangle is 1024x64px (Width,Height) in size, and the top left is at 0,0.
  * The middle rectangle is twice the size at 2048x128px in size, and the top left is at 0,128
  * The bottom rectangle is twice the size of the middle at 4096x256px in size, and the top left is at 0,360
